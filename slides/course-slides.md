# HTML Full Course — Slide Deck
Muhammad Shifat — Instructor slides
---
## Lesson 1 — HTML Basics
- Purpose of HTML: structure, not style
- Document skeleton: `<!doctype html>`, `<html>`, `<head>`, `<body>`
- Key tags: headings, paragraphs, strong/em
- Quick demo: create `basic.html` and open in browser
---
## Lesson 2 — Text & Inline Elements
- Block vs inline
- Semantic inline tags: `strong`, `em`, `abbr`, `mark`, `code`, `q`
- Replace presentational tags (`b`, `i`) with semantics
- Exercise: annotate abbreviations
---
## Lesson 3 — Links & Images
- Links: descriptive text; `target="_blank"` + `rel="noopener noreferrer"`
- Images: `src`, `alt`, decorative `alt=""`
- Responsive images: `srcset`, `sizes`, `loading="lazy"`
---
## Lesson 4 — Lists & Tables
- When to use lists vs tables
- Lists: `ul`, `ol`, `dl` and nesting
- Tables: `table`, `caption`, `thead`, `tbody`, `th scope` for accessibility
---
## Lesson 5 — Forms & Inputs
- Form basics: `form`, `label`, `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `button`
- Accessibility: `label for`, `fieldset`/`legend`
- HTML5 validation: `required`, `pattern`, `min`/`max`
---
## Lesson 6 — Semantic HTML & ARIA
- Landmarks: `header`, `nav`, `main`, `article`, `aside`, `footer`
- ARIA: use only when necessary (`aria-label`, `aria-hidden`, `aria-live`)
- Test with keyboard and devtools
---
## Lesson 7 — Media: Audio & Video
- `<audio>` and `<video>` with `controls`
- Captions: `<track kind="captions">` + WebVTT
- Provide transcripts and avoid autoplay
---
## Lesson 8 — DOM & HTML APIs
- The DOM: tree model
- Selectors: `getElementById`, `querySelector(All)`
- Manipulation: `textContent`, `innerHTML`, `createElement`, `appendChild`
- Events: `addEventListener`, `preventDefault`
---
## Lesson 9 — CSS Basics for HTML
- Attach styles: inline, internal, external
- Selectors: type, class, id, combinators
- Box model, `box-sizing`, `margin`/`padding`/`border`
---
## Lesson 10 — Flexbox & Grid
- Flexbox for 1D layout: `display:flex`, `justify-content`, `align-items`
- Grid for 2D: `display:grid`, `grid-template-columns/rows`, `gap`
- Use DevTools visualizers
---
## Lesson 11 — Responsive Design
- Mobile-first approach
- Media queries and relative units (`em`,`rem`,`vw`)
- Breakpoints and testing on devices
---
## Lesson 12 — Accessibility Best Practices
- Run Lighthouse/axe audits
- Keyboard accessibility and visible focus
- Contrast ratios and meaningful alt text
---
## Lesson 13 — SEO & Performance Basics
- Semantic HTML for SEO: titles, meta, headings
- Performance: optimize images, lazy-load, defer scripts
- Measure with Lighthouse (FCP, LCP, CLS)
---
## Lesson 14 — Projects & Portfolio
- Project ideas: portfolio, shop list, blog template, TODO app
- Deployment: GitHub Pages, Netlify
- Rubric: semantics, accessibility, responsiveness
---
## Lesson 15 — Assessments & Quizzes
- Mix MCQs and practical tasks
- Practical: fix broken HTML, build responsive navbar
- Rubric weights: semantics/accessibility 30% etc.
---
## Lesson 16 — Resources & Next Steps
- MDN, W3C ARIA practices, freeCodeCamp
- Tools: VS Code, DevTools, Squoosh
- Next topics: advanced CSS, frameworks, server basics
